DESIGN NOTE

A is doing two jobs from GAMES.md §5 — tap counterbalances left, hold cashes out. Play it once and you will feel the bug: every panicked left-correction starts a cash-out timer. Worth resolving before you build the real one.

Second one, deliberate: the position is a long, so only the left edge kills. The right edge is a rail you lean on — you cannot be liquidated by profit. And cranking mid-walk re-prices the whole beam, which is honest to “crank to add position size” but makes the walker jump. Crank from ×1 to ×8 while walking and watch what happens.
