WHAT THIS ACTUALLY IS

A one-touch (knock-in) option: does price touch +X% at any point inside 60 seconds? It pays the instant it touches and expires worthless if it never does. The power meter is the most natural interface anyone has put on an exotic, which is the whole argument in GAMES.md §3Ⓢ.

Settlement reads the true price path. Your screen only draws the ticks you bought. On a 1 Hz feed the market can spike through your barrier and back between two samples — you get paid and never see why. Those are marked unseen touch on the chart.
